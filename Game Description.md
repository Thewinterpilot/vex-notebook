## Field Elements
- 56 Cups
- 63 Pins
	- Red/Blue
	- Red/Yellow
	- Blue/Yellow
	- Yellow/Yellow
- 9 Goals
	- 4 Neutral 
	- 1 Tall
	- 2 Per Alliance
- 4 Loaders
	- 2 Per Alliance
- 4 Toggles
## How to Score
1. If a Pin is placed on top of a goal then any of its faces uncovered by cups are considered "scored"
2. Yellow Pins are worth 0 until they are claimed by turning the toggle in its respective quadrant. Whichever colour on the toggle faces the field decides how the yellow Pins in its quadrant are scored
3. Most points scored by the end of the autonomous wins a bonus of 12 points
4. Any robot breaking the plane of the midfield at the end of the game is worth 8 additional points
5. Yellow Pins on the centre goal score for whichever alliance has the most robots breaking the midfield plane

| Autonomous Bonus                 | 12 Points |
| -------------------------------- | --------- |
| Each Placed Alliance-colored Pin | 5 Points  |
| Each Owned yellow Pin            | 10 Points |
| Each Robot in the Midfield       | 8 Points  |
