# Problem:
the front meccanum wheels were originally on the inside front of the robot but we need more space so the robot can slot in with the base of the goals.

# Scratchpad:
- gear the wheels and put the motors above the wheels
- sprockets and chains
- bevel gears

# Feasibility analysis:
## gear the wheels and put the motors above the wheels
reasonable but takes a lot of vertical real estate in the front of the robot (important space) to mount the motor directly above the wheels
## sprockets and chains
chosen idea
places motors in less valuable space, saves the required amount of space inside the middle of the robot
# bevel gears
takes about the same amount of space as mounting the motors directly to the wheels
