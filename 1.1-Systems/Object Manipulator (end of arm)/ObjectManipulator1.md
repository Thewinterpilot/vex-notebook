#winter #object-manipulation
## object manipulator base note
## design requirements:
- be able to flip pieces over
- fit on the end of the arm
## idea proposals:
- 180 degree rotating claw
- rollers
- new Zealand intake

## decision:
180 degree rotating modified NZ intake

## reasoning:
any claw would be harder to grab multiple game objects with,
a normal NZ intake would not pick things up well,
rollers are just nonviable for this game
## later decision refinement
(Nov 17 2026)
upon further research we realised that rollers are actually totally viable and so we're going to use those
we made two rows of wheels that grip on either side of a game piece
## well that's getting walked back
we're just doing a claw, there was a miscommunication about how much space we had in the middle of the lift, we're switching again and making a claw.
