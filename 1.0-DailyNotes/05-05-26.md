**Our decision so far:**
We want to do a mecanum/omni wheel hybrid. 6 4-inch wheels, 3 a side, mecanum in the corners and omni in the middle. We chose this because it gives us the manoeuvrability of omnidirectional motion, and the pushing power and grip of a 6-wheel drivetrain. We want to have one 5.5W motor per mecanum wheel, and one 11W motor per omni wheel. This adheres to the 44W limit we decided on to allow enough motors for the peripherals.
The next stage is to start designing the drivetrain layout and deciding on dimensions and wheel placement.
