## how to move game objects vertically
we were thinking double-reverse 4bar
most people seem to be doing cascade lifts, that seems to be working well.

### pros/cons of cascade lift
#### pros:
- more compact footprint
- more stable
- lighter
#### cons:
- more delicate
- high maintanence
- probably slower
- strings suck to use even when they hold
- moving down at speed might be harder

### pros/cons of scissor lift
#### pros:
- fast vertical movement
#### cons:
- low space efficiency
- unstable
- low lifting power
