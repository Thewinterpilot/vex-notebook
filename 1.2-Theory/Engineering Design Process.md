 The engineering design process is a set of (general) steps engineers follow when designing a product or system.
 
 there are seven steps, (they may be named other things but) they're generally:
 * Identify the task (Ask): figure out what you need and what the constraints are.
 * Scratchpad: (Research) brainstorm solutions the Problem, write why you like different ideas, there are no bad ideas in this step.
 * research solutions online (useful to see how other people solved similar problems)
 * Plan by picking a solution you think will work and writing a *detailed* summary of it
 * create a prototype of that design or a detailed drawing of it
 * test prototype if you made one or just go through possible points of faliure
 * improve and redesign if you find problems
 * implement
